# Purpose & Vision
This document encapsulates the purpose of the Capture app, and it's vision for how it will fulfill that purpose.

Capture's purpose is built right into its name, it is aid the user in capturing information important to them.

There are many applications that already do this so what makes Capture stand our among the others? 

## Ownership
All the data that goes into Capture lives on their device in a folder of their choosing.
It does not store anything important to the user outside a proper, human-readable, file.

## Plain Text
Capture is file driven and being able to load, edit, and save plain text files is it's core strength.
This is really important on platforms like Android that do not have a plain text editor built in.

## Financial Transaction Tracking
Besides quick notes, the primary piece of information that is beneficial to capture is our daily transactions.
Capture offers an entire system for logging transactions into CSV files.
But the user does not have to interact with the CSV file directly, Capture manages that complexity for the user.

## Simplicity
Because Capture, at the end of the day, is just operating on simple files the data ultimately remains simple.
A Human can open any file Capture creates or uses and read it.

## Portability
Plain files are portable!
Portability of the files means they can go anywhere and be transformed easily.

## Flexibility
Databases have their place, but you need an entire system to manipulate them and they are opaque.
Plain files can be used by tons of other software too.

## Multiplatform
Capture works on Android and Desktop Environments (iOS, and Web may come later).

## AI
AI is quickly making it's way into every digital surface of our lives and Capture is not ignoring its value.
Where others are integrating and relying heavily on remote AI servers, Capture is taking a different approach.
Capture is pioneering the idea that local, on device, AI models are the future.
Local AI models are **free** to use, and free is a core offering of Capture.
